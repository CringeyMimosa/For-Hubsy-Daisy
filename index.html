dear aisy,

to my loveliest and strongest husband, happy birthday! may you always be blessed with happiness, good health and everything beautiful in life.

this is your first birthday after we got married, and only God knows how happy i am to celebrate this special day beside you, this time as your wife. aisy, i’m sorry that i can’t afford to do much other than what i’ve managed to prepare for you on this sweetest day of yours. it’s something i thought could make you happy, and i genuinely hope you appreciate this customized wish and your birthday treat from me. it’s not much, but it’s all from my heart :)

aisy, i love you, and nothing can ever change that. a million times over, i’d still choose you. i hope we can have a happy marriage together, both you and me. we’re going to create a happy little world of our own someday, just like we’ve always dreamed of.

i know you’re trying your best for us. i know how hard you’re working for the sake of our comfort and future. i see your efforts, aisy, and i hope you know that i appreciate everything you do, even the little things that you might think go unnoticed.

throughout this journey, we’ve had lots of ups and downs, cries and laughter, good days and difficult days. but i guess that’s the beauty of love, isn’t it? we learn to accept each other’s flaws and weaknesses because we’re not perfect, and neither is our relationship. what matters is that we keep trying, keep understanding each other and keep choosing one another. i hope we never stop doing that.

sometimes, when i think about everything we’ve been through, i can’t help but wonder how we ended up here. all the little moments, the unexpected turns and everything that happened along the way, just so the world could bring us together and intertwine our lives. perhaps we were always meant to find each other.

aisy, one of my biggest prayers is for God to grant you a long, healthy and happy life. i pray that He protects you wherever you go, keeps you safe from harm and always watches over you, especially when i’m not around. you know how worried i get every time you go out. sometimes, i get scared just thinking about the possibility of losing you. i’m afraid of a life where i no longer get to see you, hug you, annoy you or simply have you around. i know that everything is in God’s hands, and all i can do is pray that He keeps you safe and stays by your side at all times.

i pray that we’re given a long life together. i want us to grow old together, experience everything we’ve been dreaming of and witness all the little milestones that are yet to come. i hope we get to spend as many years together as God allows us to. and if there’s one thing i wish for, it’s that we never have to experience the pain of losing one another too soon. i can’t imagine having to live without you, and i wouldn’t want you to ever have to go through that kind of pain either.

and aisy, please look at me as your precious wife, just as i look at you as my precious husband. i want to feel loved by you, too. i want your love, your attention and your affection, just as much as i’m always trying to pour mine into you. sometimes, i miss you even when you’re right beside me. i don’t know how to explain it, but i just want to be close to you. i want your hugs, your kisses, your attention and all those little things that remind me i’m loved by you. let’s never get too comfortable with each other that we forget to show how much we love one another.

let’s also learn to be kinder to ourselves and to the people around us. let’s be patient with each other, understand each other better and give each other room to grow. we’re still learning how to be husband and wife, and there’s so much more for us to experience together. i hope we continue growing as individuals while building a beautiful marriage along the way.

i want to be with you forever, and i hope you do too. let’s keep dreaming about the future we’ve always wanted, where we get to live peacefully and happily, in our own little world. i want to grow old with you, hold your hand through everything and still be just as excited to come home to you after all these years.

happy birthday once again, my love. i pray that life treats you kindly, that your dreams slowly become reality and that you always have reasons to smile. whatever happens along the way, i hope we never forget that we’re in this together.

i love you, aisy. a million times, i’d choose you. always.

love,

your wife ♡

Written with all my heart
